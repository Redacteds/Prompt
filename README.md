# Image Prompt Notes

As of 2026-10-26

## Prompt 1: orange-lens editorial portrait

This prompt asks for a minimal fashion-editorial portrait of a smiling man on a bright white backdrop.

- Wardrobe: oversized black sunglasses with orange lenses, a neon blue hoodie, small gold earrings.
- Lighting: bright studio light, rim light outlining the face, soft vignette edges.
- Style: Vogue-cover editorial look, clean and vibrant, 8K studio quality, HDR minimalist aesthetic.
- Reference: the prompt says to keep the same face as a reference photo. Use only a photo of someone who has agreed to it.

Open question: is this for a cover mock-up, or for a general style reference?

## Prompt 2: inflatable Google Drive logo

This prompt asks for a soft, air-filled 3D version of the Google Drive triangle logo on a transparent background.

- Shape and colour: keep the logo's shape and proportions, with the original green, yellow and blue. No recolouring.
- Material: fabric or vinyl-like surface with visible stitching, soft creases and fine grain.
- Lighting and composition: studio light, soft shadows, centred and slightly angled, no text or background.
- Output: ultra-high resolution, product-style render, PNG with transparency.

Open question: is the logo for internal use or public use? Brand-use rules may apply to the logo.

## Summary and reuse notes

Both prompts use the same pattern: a subject, a material or style, lighting, composition, and a quality target.

- Keep the subject and brand rules in the first line.
- Name the lighting and background explicitly.
- End with a quality target such as resolution or render style.
